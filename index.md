    <p>Hi there! I'm a PhD Candidate in Social and Political Science at Bocconi University in Milan. I'm currently working remotely from Rotterdam, the Netherlands. </p>
    <p> My research focuses on political behavior and public opinion in Western Europe, and sometimes beyond. Most of my current work examines the consequences of far-right parties and politicians' increased visibility and success. In my dissertation I show that far-right success can lead to more hostility toward minorities and rule-breaking in parliament.</p> 
    <p>In other work, I study changing party electorates in Western Europe, and I have assembled new data on speeches from parliamentary committee debates in the Netherlands (~800.000 speeches.) My research has been published in the <em>British Journal of Political Science</em>.</p>
